**Goal**: As a visitor to Noel's portfolio, I want to understand his integrated technology-and-growth profile and verify all ten career experiences, so that I can quickly assess his relevant experience while the existing portfolio experience is preserved.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Requirements**: PROF-01, PROF-02, SKIL-01, EXPR-01, EXPR-02, EXPR-03, EXPR-04, EXPR-05, PRES-01
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. The page title, meta description, and landing headline identify Noel with an integrated technology-and-growth focus rather than presenting only a single role or creative discipline.
  2. Visitors can read an updated about summary spanning AI automation and integration, marketing operations and data, technical support and client-facing solutions, software development, and creative work, and find skills representing those areas, without unsupported claims.
  3. Visitors can find all supplied experience entries with their source-confirmed titles and dates: Joseph Engineering Services (Digital Marketing Executive / Data Manager, Nov 2025–Present); freelance software engineering / AI integration / automation (Mar 2023–Present); Exacot (Mar 2023–Mar 2025); FCC Supermarket (Mar 2022–Feb 2023); Majorel / AppleCare (Feb 2021–Mar 2022); Sitel Group (Nov 2019–Jan 2021); Sutherland (Oct 2019–Sep 2020); 4N Internet Café and Printing Services (Manager / Owner, Sep 2015–Jan 2020); freelance content creation (Sep 2015–Present); and self-employed product ownership (Mar 2023–Present). Sitel and Sutherland remain visibly concurrent, and unconfirmed CV placeholders are not published as facts.
  4. Every role with duties confirmed in the supplied CVs has a concise summary supported by those source details; roles without confirmed duties show only the confirmed role, organization, and dates.
  5. Visitors can still use the existing navigation and contact options, view the existing portfolio galleries, and use the site's responsive presentation; the content update does not introduce a redesign or remove existing sections.
